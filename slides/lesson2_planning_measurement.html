Planning and measurement

Every technique must discharge a named verification claim.

Plan the claim→Model what is sampled→Close holes honestly
    HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 21

    Open with a decision the audience must make in this area. Ask which result they currently treat as sufficient evidence and keep that answer for the closing discussion.

    The lesson selects a practical argument from the book. The study handout preserves the detailed examples, citations and worked artifacts.

    Planning and measurement

    Learning outcomes

    Demonstrate these decisions on a real plan row.

    • Write a plan row with one closure criterion.
    • Design sampling around the event of interest.
    • Classify a hole with a reviewable reason.
    • Explain a readiness decision using correlated evidence.
    HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 22

    Read the outcomes as observable tasks. Ask learners to identify which one they can already demonstrate and which one currently depends on undocumented judgment.

    At closure, require a concrete artifact or explanation for an outcome rather than a general expression of confidence.

    Planning and measurement

    Route through the lesson

    Four blocks, each ending with a decision.

    • Plan the claim
    • Model what is sampled
    • Close holes honestly
    • Read the evidence together
    HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 23

    Allow eighteen minutes for each block: a concept, its engineering consequence, a worked interpretation and a short discussion. Keep the discussion tied to the evidence available in the example.

    Reserve the final eleven minutes for the assigned exercise and application to the learners’ work. The timing is a course design allocation, not an engineering estimate.

    Planning and measurement

    Extract behavior, not headings

    Omissions in the plan become omissions in verification.

    • Read interfaces, functions and architecture.
    • Record forbidden and out-of-scope behavior.
    HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 24

    Walk a DMA description from register interface to transfer behavior and then to architectural interactions. A section heading is not yet an independently checkable feature.

    Ask what the designer knows about corner cases that the prose does not say. Keep the must-not-happen list explicit; a happy-path feature list can be complete in appearance and still omit the escape.

    Planning and measurement

    A row needs one closure argument

    Separate claims that require different evidence.

    Feature and attributes→Pass/fail criterion→Method and closure
      HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 25

      Use a protocol property and a functional transfer result to illustrate why one feature description can need separate rows. The checker and closure metric must match the kind of wrong behavior.

      Require the criterion before choosing the method. A test name or the phrase fully verified does not specify what will be observed or what discharges the row.

      Planning and measurement

      Keep traceability executable

      Stable identifiers connect intent to results.

      • Link specification, feature, tests and coverage.
      • Make missing links visible in review.
      HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 26

      Trace one permanent feature ID into the regression selection and back from its result. The direction matters: the report must identify which claim was checked, not merely which executable ran.

      Invite design, systems, test and outside peers to challenge correctness, precision and completeness. Explain that a tool can preserve links but cannot detect an important feature nobody entered.

      Planning and measurement

      Discussion: one row, two verdicts

      Name the evidence that would settle the decision.

      • A row claims legal bursts and correct copied data.
      • Its only criterion is an assertion result.
      • How should the plan change?
      HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 27

      Expected answer: separate the protocol-legality claim from the data-correctness claim, assigning the appropriate property and functional comparison.

      The distinction is evidence, not bureaucracy. A single passing protocol result cannot discharge a different data claim merely because both concern the DMA.

      Planning and measurement

      Coverage families have different subjects

      A full structural report can miss absent functionality.

      Implementation→Specification→Checker activity
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 28

        Ask where each model comes from. Code coverage is derived from implementation structure; functional coverage is authored from intended behavior; assertion activity describes whether checkers were exercised.

        An omitted implementation cannot contribute code to cover. Use that observation to explain why structural completeness and specification completeness are different review targets.

        Planning and measurement

        Bins belong at behavior boundaries

        Convenient partitions can merge cases that behave differently.

        • Choose attributes from the specification.
        • Shape crosses to justified combinations.
        • Sample attributes when they describe the same event.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 29

        Use the accelerator example: width, precision and contention matter because they select different behavior. Equal-sized automatic bins need not respect those boundaries.

        Ask when the attributes become jointly valid. A correct list of attributes sampled at unrelated times produces a misleading model even when its report looks complete.

        Planning and measurement

        Sampling is not checking

        A filled bin needs an active correctness oracle.

        • Coverage records that a modeled situation occurred.
        • A checker determines whether its behavior was acceptable.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 210

        Present a campaign optimized for bin hits. Ask what connects each hit to a mechanism that would reject a wrong result. The optimization target can be satisfied while that mechanism is disconnected.

        Require coverage-model review to include the observing checkers. Do not replace this review with a comparison count: activity is necessary evidence, not a proof of checker correctness.

        Planning and measurement

        Discussion: contention at the wrong instant

        Name the evidence that would settle the decision.

        • Contention is sampled only when a job retires.
        • The defect requires contention during streaming.
        • What must the model remember?
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 211

        Expected answer: latch or otherwise preserve whether contention occurred during the relevant streaming window, then sample attributes together at a defined completion event.

        Explain why the same report can be produced by a good and bad sampling model. The semantic error lives in the relation between the recorded attributes and the event of interest.

        Planning and measurement

        A coverage hole is a diagnosis

        Different causes require different actions.

        • Check stimulus reachability and model intent.
        • Distinguish unexercised behavior from impossible behavior.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 212

        Follow a hole through classification before proposing more seeds. A model mistake, a generator restriction and a rare reachable case require different fixes.

        Keep formal unreachability claims tied to their assumptions. A constraint in the stimulus library is not automatically a restriction of the design’s legal behavior.

        Planning and measurement

        An exclusion is a reviewable artifact

        The report must retain the reason a hole was removed.

        • Classify the exclusion.
        • Record reason, owner and relevant version.
        • Review it when the design or plan changes.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 213

        Distinguish structural restrictions, specification restrictions and accepted risk. Each carries a different justification and authority.

        Ask which change would invalidate the reason. A waiver copied from a previous configuration is an assumption that needs re-examination, not permanent evidence.

        Planning and measurement

        Risk determines depth

        Good observability makes checking affordable; it does not remove risk.

        • Consider consequence, novelty and specification quality.
        • Agree priority and cut lines before the schedule crisis.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 214

        Contrast the new DMA stride path with generated, well-specified register behavior. The chapter spends verification effort according to escape consequence and likelihood.

        When a specification is incomplete, record zones and explicit defects. A local interpretation embedded in code must not become an unreviewed requirement merely because implementation has begun.

        Planning and measurement

        Discussion: unreachable or just ungenerated?

        Name the evidence that would settle the decision.

        • A legal bin has never been hit.
        • The sequence library does not generate it.
        • Is a structural exclusion justified?
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 215

        Expected answer: no. The observation establishes a stimulus limitation, not structural impossibility. Either expand stimulus or record a separately justified risk decision.

        Ask what would justify unreachability: a specification restriction or a proof under reviewed assumptions. Have learners state the difference in the exclusion’s reason field.

        Planning and measurement

        A dashboard is a set of instruments

        No single curve determines readiness.

        • Read per-feature status with coverage trends.
        • Correlate bug curves, regression health and debug effort.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 216

        Treat the plan as the organizing measure and use the other instruments to challenge its interpretation. A dashboard should trigger a decision, not simply decorate a status report.

        Ask whether the same apparent improvement could be caused by reduced stimulus, disabled checking or a changing denominator. State the companion measurement that would discriminate.

        Planning and measurement

        A flat bug curve is ambiguous

        Clean behavior and stimulus starvation can look alike.

        • Check freshness and breadth of stimulus.
        • Inspect checker integrity and where recent bugs were found.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 217

        Offer two explanations for flat discovery and ask the room to distinguish them using available data. The chapter requires fresh-stimulus and coverage information rather than inferring quality from the curve alone.

        A late severe bug in an unmodeled region changes the model and the plan. Demonstrate stability again under the expanded instrumentation; the old flat curve did not watch that region.

        Planning and measurement

        Sign-off is an evidence decision

        Conditional release must name the accepted gap.

        • Discharge plan rows and review exclusions.
        • Record reproducible baselines and bug dispositions.
        • A conditional decision needs an owner and expiry.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 218

        Use the chapter’s three possible outcomes: signed off, conditional or held. Ask learners to distinguish a conditional decision from an informal promise to fix something later.

        The waiver names the hole, reason, risk argument, owner and expiry. The record must permit someone outside the meeting to reconstruct what was accepted.

        Planning and measurement

        Discussion: a late severe bug

        Name the evidence that would settle the decision.

        • Discovery had been flat.
        • A new checker finds a severe unmodeled failure.
        • Which earlier conclusion must be revisited?
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 219

        Expected answer: the old stability evidence did not observe the newly discovered region. Update the plan, coverage and checking as necessary, then establish the required evidence under the changed instrumentation.

        Do not treat fixing the immediate RTL defect as completing the escape analysis. The process that allowed the missing observation must also produce an upstream change.

        Planning and measurement

        Exercise: coverage without comparisons

        Decide what the report can establish.

        • The modeled space is fully hit.
        • No output comparison was performed.
        • State the missing evidence and a defensible closure decision.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 220

        Exercise 6.3 (judgement). A closure tool reads the uncovered bins back from a merged database, converts them mechanically into constraints, and brings the accelerator's model from a plateau to full coverage overnight. Decide what that result establishes about the campaign and what it does not, and name the property of coverage closure §6.4 states that makes the distinction necessary. List the stopping criteria of §6.8 the result does not by itself satisfy, and give the reason §6.8 offers for tightening the checking review when closure is automated.

        Use book Exercise 6.3. Give learners time individually, then compare answers in pairs before the debrief.

        Expected answer: the report measures sampling across an authored model, not correctness or completeness of that model. Closure needs a reviewed full model, current exclusions and stability under refinement, together with active checking of behavior. Reject an unconditional closure based on bin hits alone.

        Planning and measurement

        What to change on Monday

        Make one existing verification decision reviewable.

        • Review one row with mixed closure criteria.
        • Trace a coverage hit to its checker.
        • Challenge one exclusion or dashboard inference.
        HARDWARE VERIFICATION LESSON 221

        Ask each learner to choose one of these actions in an existing project. Require a small, inspectable deliverable and identify the person who will review it.

        Keep the change connected to the lesson’s evidence limits. A new dashboard, script or model is useful only if it strengthens the claim the plan row is meant to establish.
